Step 1
Initial Detection & Acknowledgement

Formally recognize the incident before reporting begins. Detection can happen through human observation, automated security tools, or directly from the threat actor.

Step 2
Preliminary Analysis

Assess the scope and determine the potential blast radius of the incident. Apply the proper categorization and severity levels (P1-P4) to dictate response priority.

Step 3
Incident Logging

Meticulously track every action taken and piece of evidence found using tools like TheHive Project or JIRA. Pen and paper act as fallbacks.

Step 4
Notification of Relevant Parties

Promptly alert stakeholders based on the incident’s scope. Internal communications to IT/Legal/PR, and external to regulatory bodies or customers.

Step 5
Detailed Investigation & Reporting

Conduct a comprehensive technical analysis. Compile all findings, logs, and evidence to understand the full impact.

Step 6
Final Report Creation

Create the definitive record of the event detailing what happened, the root cause, and the remedial actions taken for executives and regulators.

Step 7
Feedback Loop

Revisit the event in a post-incident reflection. Identify what went wrong and how to improve the response process, turning the incident into a strategic asset.
